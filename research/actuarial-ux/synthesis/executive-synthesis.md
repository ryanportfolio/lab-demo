# Executive synthesis

Four blind, independent desk-research passes (2026-08-07, same model/day — see independence caveat in [README.md](README.md)) reconciled into 25 canonical findings. CAN references: [canonical-findings.md](canonical-findings.md).

## Strongest established findings

1. **The real incumbent is a socio-technical lash-up, not a product.** Spreadsheets plus a heavyweight specialist platform, held together by file-name versioning ("Model_Final_v23.xlsm"), Excel changelog tabs, shared drives, and email review (CAN-06 strong, CAN-07 strong). Versioning is literally a human job duty in AXIS job postings. This is the best-corroborated workflow fact in the campaign: professional-body, vendor-survey, and implementation-artifact classes agree.

2. **Latency is normalized at three layers, and fidelity pays for it.** Model build/fill-in (week+ for ~half — vendor-fingerprinted), compute (overnight grid runs sold as a feature; ~88% scenario cuts; simplify-to-meet-reporting-schedule; a decade of SOA runtime research; senior FSAs hand-optimizing loop bounds), and deployment (months-long IT re-coding into rating engines) (CAN-08 strong, CAN-09, CAN-10, CAN-11 moderate). Practitioners don't complain — they batch questions, ration scenarios, and call it methodology. The foregone fidelity leaves no record.

3. **Regulation is widening the gap tooling created.** ASOP 56 makes model understanding an individual duty; the NAIC AI bulletin (~24 states) demands documented AI-systems programs with a 12-state exam pilot live in 2026; the FCA already found model monitoring "high-level summaries with little substance" (CAN-18 strong, CAN-19, CAN-20). Demanded: reproducibility on demand. Recorded by the toolchain: filenames and email chains.

4. **Prediction Lab is real, fast-shipping, and aimed at exactly this gap — on vendor evidence only.** Weekly releases Feb–Jul 2026; review diffs, coefficient-delta comparisons, AI modeling agents (v1.30.0, 2026-06-10); enterprise-procurement posture first (single-tenant, BYOC, SOC 2) (CAN-01–03). **Zero independent customer/outcome evidence exists (CAN-04, 4/4 runs), the app is uninspectable (CAN-05), and no visible text describes human approval checkpoints for agents (CAN-02).**

5. **Switching cost is people.** Platform-specific FSA specialist roles at $163–200k, hiring decided by Prophet experience, single reserving roles spanning five tools (CAN-15 strong, CAN-21).

## Strategic tensions

- **X1:** deployment pain is real but not universal — G2 reviewers praise Radar Live deployment; pain segments by tooling generation.
- **X2:** Excel and closed systems have defended virtues (formula reviewability, junior-team consistency); root cause may be the actuary–IT org split, and replacements risk relocating shadow IT rather than fixing it.
- **X3:** headline dissatisfaction magnitudes (96–99%) all trace to one vendor's survey family; independent channels that could verify are dead or blocked.
- **X4:** the best-quantified pain is life-side compute; Prediction Lab's visible feature depth is P&C-pricing-shaped (inference). The loudest pain and the apparent wedge may be different markets.
- **Agent trust gate:** ~half of practitioners still fear AI (vendor-fingerprinted, falling); signing actuaries must honestly claim model understanding. Agent-run experiments without examiner-grade traces are professionally unsignable — this is the make-or-break UX surface, not a feature.

## Most consequential unknowns

1. How model evidence actually reaches committees and filings (zero observational evidence; 3 runs flag it as the top gap).
2. Whether signing actuaries and 2026 exam pilots will accept agent-executed modeling, and in what trace format.
3. Anything at all about Prediction Lab's real-world behavior.
4. Vendor-independent, current time-use numbers.
