# Coverage gaps

What four same-day, same-model, same-toolchain desk passes could NOT see. Gaps are correlated across runs (shared access constraints), so "4 runs agree" never closed any of these.

## Source classes missing entirely
- **Direct practitioner voice:** Reddit fetch-blocked (all runs), Actuarial Outpost defunct, GoActuary JS-gated (one 2020 post recovered), acted.co.uk empty, archive.org blocked. Entire campaign contains ONE forum post.
- **Hands-on product inspection:** zero — Prediction Lab login-gated (WorkOS) + trust page 403 + no public docs; incumbents license-gated (Prophet, AXIS, Radar, Emblem, ResQ, Arius, Earnix, hx Renew all uninspected).
- **Support tickets / telemetry:** never public; no proxy found.
- **Interviews / observation:** out of scope for desk research; the highest-ranked open questions all require them.
- **Neutral quantitative surveys (current):** best neutral numbers are 2008 (GIRO data quality) and 2021 (Oliver Wyman); everything newer is vendor-commissioned.

## Populations and segments unrepresented
- Satisfied incumbent users (structurally silent — no channel).
- Small insurers and mutuals; MGAs partially (hx sample skews specialty/London).
- Health actuaries; pension/retirement actuaries.
- Non-US/UK geographies (one UK regulatory thread is the only non-US extension; no EU Solvency II practitioner evidence, no APAC).
- Junior analysts vs signing actuaries — no evidence distinguishes their needs.
- Underwriters/business consumers of model output (one vendor-survey stat only, CAN-13).

## Products with thin or zero evidence
- ResQ, Arius, Earnix, Emblem UI specifics: near-zero direct evidence (marketing + one G2 page each at best).
- Prediction Lab: interior, pricing, customers, segment focus, agent permissioning — all unknown (CAN-04/05).
- Life-side applicability of any modern challenger: no evidence.

## Workflow stages under-covered
- Committee review and filing assembly — how charts/diagnostics/exact values travel into decisions (flagged by 3 runs as the biggest inference gap; zero observational evidence).
- Assumption-setting and experience-study workflows.
- Failed-experiment memory / exploration history (no literature found at all).
- Learnability/onboarding (RC noted its own lens was thin here).

## Temporal gaps
- Current (2025–26) time-use numbers: none neutral.
- hx survey editions 2023–2025 conflated in places across runs; edition-level claims not always separable.
